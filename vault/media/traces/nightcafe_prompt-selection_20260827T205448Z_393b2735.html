Frequently Asked Questions about AI Art and NightCafe

🎨 How do I create AI Generated Art?

On NightCafe, creating AI generated art is as simple as typing your prompt and clicking "Create". Start by trying a few prompts, then try using a few different models to get a feel for what you like. AI art is all about experimentation and creativity!

❄️ What makes NightCafe unique?

NightCafe is an AI art generator, but what makes it unique is the community. Join millions of other AI art enthusiasts and publish your creations, like and comment on other creations, hang out in AI art chat rooms and even participate in the official daily AI Art Challenge - all without leaving NightCafe.

✨ Is AI Art a good creative hobby?

Yes! Most NightCafe users are hobbyists who create for fun. Many NightCafe users turn to AI art to unwind, express themselves, and feel better. It's a low-pressure creative outlet with no gatekeepers: just you, your imagination, and a supportive community cheering you on.

💸 Does it cost money to use NightCafe?

No! Millions of people use NightCafe without ever paying a cent. Everyone gets free credits every day, and non-paying users have access to a wide range of models. Users who go "PRO" get more credits, and access to more powerful models, but many users find the free plan is plenty for them.

🛠️ Does NightCafe have any unique features?

NightCafe's official daily AI Art Challenge is a unique feature that makes it stand out from other AI art generators. There's a new theme every day, which can help you overcome creative blocks and get inspired.

🧠 What AI models are on NightCafe?

NightCafe offers more models than any other AI art platform—including Flux, Stable Diffusion, DALL·E 3, Google Imagen, Gemini, Ideogram, HiDream, Seedream, plus video models like Runway, Kling, Seedance, and more. New models get added all the time!

📱 What platforms does NightCafe work on?

NightCafe's AI art generator works on the web, and can be installed to the home screen of your iPhone, Android phone or tablet. Your NightCafe account works across your devices, and all your creations are saved to your account.

💼 Can I use my AI creations for commercial purposes?

Yes! Even on the free plan! As long as you own (or have permission to use) any original assets (e.g. reference images) used to create the artwork, we transfer any Copyright assignment to you - the image creator. You own the creations and can use them for commercial purposes. Please check the Copyright laws in your own country to confirm. Copyright laws for AI-generated art are still evolving, and some jurisdictions will not grant any Copyright ownership at all.

☄️ What is a streak?

A streak is the number of consecutive days that a user has created art on NightCafe. Some NightCafe users have created something every day for over 3 years!
